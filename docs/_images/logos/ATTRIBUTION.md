# Provider logo attribution

Every logo in this directory is the respective organization's own mark, used only to visually
identify which provider a backend talks to (nominative fair use), not to imply endorsement of
`earthlens` by that organization. Trademarks and service marks remain the property of their
respective owners.

Three providers have no logo here because no distinct official mark could be found:
`admin` (geoBoundaries — text/CSS branding only), `ghsl` (Joint Research Centre GHSL — only
generic European Commission/Copernicus branding exists), and `nwm` (NOAA National Water
Model — only generic NOAA/NWS branding exists).

One rename note: **NREL** (`nrel`) was renamed by the US Department of Energy to the
**National Laboratory of the Rockies** effective 2025-12-01; `nrel.gov` no longer resolves
and the org's current site is `nlr.gov`. The `nrel` backend id is unchanged (it matches the
package's existing code), but the displayed organization name and logo reflect the new name.
